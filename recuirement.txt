1. Objective & Core Purpose
The Problem: Researchers and students find it time-consuming to manually review and compare findings/limitations across multiple papers to recognize common or overlapping research gaps

The Solution: An AI-powered agent that ingests a collection of abstracts or summaries, synthesizes their limitations, and generates a structured, cross-referenced report of potential research gaps strictly supported by the provided text

2. Input Requirements
The agent must accept the following inputs

Research Paper Abstracts/Summaries (Required): A small collection of text blocks containing the abstract or summary of each paper
.
Paper Identifiers (Required): A unique key for each paper (such as title, author, year, or short ID like Paper 1, Paper 2) to handle cross-referencing and source attribution
.
Research Domain (Optional): The broad subject area (e.g., Machine Learning in Healthcare, Artificial Intelligence, or Education)
.
Focus Area (Optional): A specific topic or problem the user wants the agent to focus its analysis on
.
3. Core Processing & Logic Constraints
Strict Grounding: The analysis, identified gaps, evidence, and suggestions must be based only on the supplied papers. The agent must not invent, extrapolate, or introduce external solutions or limitations
.
Synthesis & Cross-Referencing: The agent must compare findings across papers to find overlapping issues, rather than just listing limitations paper-by-paper
.
Source Attribution: Each gap must list exactly which papers support or mention it
.
Priority Assignment: The agent must assign a priority status (High, Medium, or Low) based on how frequently or severely the gap impacts the studies
.
4. Expected Output Format
The final report generated by the agent must strictly follow this structure
:
Part A: Potential Research Gaps
For each gap identified, the output must include:
Title of Research Gap: A concise header (e.g., Limited Dataset Diversity)
.
Gap Description: A clear explanation of how existing studies fall short or what they over-rely on
.
Supporting Papers: The exact paper identifiers that mention this limitation
.
Evidence: A specific excerpt or reasoning explaining how the gap was identified from the text
.
Priority: Configured strictly as High, Medium, or Low
.
Potential Research Direction: A brief suggestion for future research, grounded strictly in the source limitations
.
Part B: Exception Handling
Unclear or Insufficiently Supported Gaps (Optional): A section highlighting proposed gaps that lack sufficient evidence in the text (omit if not applicable)
.
Part C: Overall Research Gap Summary
A 1-2 sentence paragraph summarizing the most common gaps across all papers and recommending a broad path forward for future studies
.
5. Official Test Blueprint (Reference Case)
The system should be validated against the following benchmark dataset from your specification sheet
:
Domain: Machine Learning in Healthcare
Paper 1: Machine learning model for disease prediction with limitations involving small datasets and limited testing across hospitals
.
Paper 2: Deep learning for medical image analysis with limitations involving dataset diversity and generalization
.
Paper 3: AI diagnostic prediction system with limitations in interpretability and real-world validation
.
Expected Gaps Derived:
Limited Dataset Diversity (Supported by: Paper 1, Paper 2) — High Priority
.
Limited Real-World Validation (Supported by: Paper 1, Paper 3) — High Priority
.
Model Interpretability (Supported by: Paper 3) — Medium Priority
.
